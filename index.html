Overall impact narrative

Meaningful practice, with a path to sustainable scale.

LangBuddy has established meaningful student engagement across pilot schools, created opportunities for speaking practice across all three Mother Tongue Languages, and received encouraging feedback from students and teachers.

The product has demonstrated initial adoption, generated substantial recorded practice activity and shown early signs of repeat and beyond-school engagement. The next stage is to deepen engagement, strengthen the learning experience, establish evidence of educational value and build a sustainable model for wider school adoption.

Our 2027 ambition: Move LangBuddy from initial adoption towards deeper, more consistent and sustained speaking practice.

Measurement notes & definitions
  1. A meaningful session is defined as a student session lasting at least 30 seconds and containing at least 5 exchanges.
  2. The 1,689 figure represents unique students with at least one meaningful session during January–October 2026.
  3. The 14.4-minute North Star is calculated using 26,510.35 meaningful practice minutes during school terms divided by 1,841 active student-term records. Students are counted once per term in which they had meaningful usage.
  4. The 447.8 total practice hours includes school-holiday activity. The 441.8 school-term hours are used for the term-based North Star calculation.
  5. Repeat practice is the share of unique students with meaningful usage who were active in at least 2 distinct calendar weeks across January–October 2026: 503 of 1,689 students (29.8%). Weeks do not need to be consecutive.
  6. Beyond-school practice is based on transcript session-start timestamps: weekdays after 3pm and all weekends. It is a timing-based indicator, not proof of voluntary or independent practice.
  7. Language classification uses an automated second-pass analysis of student transcript utterances. Chinese, Tamil and Malay are reported separately; mixed Chinese/Tamil and mixed Malay/English sessions remain separate categories. English, unclear/multilingual/insufficient-text and insufficient/non-speech sessions are also retained separately. Classification may be affected by short utterances, code-switching and speech-to-text errors.
  8. Rollout context: Term 1 involved Sec 2 G1/G2 with structured lesson integration; Term 2 involved Sec 2 G1/G2 without structural support; Term 3 involved Sec 2 G3 with organic adoption and schools choosing when and how to use LangBuddy. Term 4 overlaps with examinations and is a partial snapshot.
  9. Term 4 figures cover 14 September–31 October 2026 and represent a partial examination-period snapshot.
  10. Recorded session duration is used as a proxy for speaking practice. It is not a direct measurement of the time students were actively speaking.
  11. The 21 pilot-school count (16 Secondary and 5 Primary) is based on the August scorecard summary and should be reconciled against the detailed school roster.
  12. The S$0.10 per-session AI token cost is an optimisation target, not an achieved cost. Token estimates exclude other operating costs.

Language classification and engagement metrics are based on the available transcript exports. Figures should be interpreted with the stated reporting windows and measurement limitations.
